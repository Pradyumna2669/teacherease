# TeacherEase Product Plan

## Objective
Turn TeacherEase from a paper-formatting + scan utility into the default way Indian teachers create exam papers quickly, with a clear path from first paper to paid repeat usage.

## Diagnosis from current product and usage
- The product already has real usage: multiple users are creating real papers and question records.
- The current homepage and pricing position TeacherEase as a formatting and scan tool, not as a complete exam-paper creation workflow.
- The current credits model is easy to understand technically, but not from a teacher's perspective.
- Teachers think in terms of “How many papers can I make?” rather than “How many credits do I have?”
- The strongest opportunity is to anchor the product around AI-assisted paper generation, then layer scanning and manual editing around it.

## Goals
1. Make the first screen communicate a clear value proposition: “Create a complete paper in minutes.”
2. Shift the product narrative from “formatting tool” to “exam paper generator.”
3. Improve activation: sign up → create a paper → preview → download → repeat.
4. Design a pricing structure that aligns with teacher behavior and the product's real use cases.
5. Build the foundations for repeat usage, answer keys, saved question banks, and school accounts.

## Strategic direction
### Core positioning
TeacherEase should sell the outcome, not the formating mechanics.

Message to test:
> Create a complete question paper in 2 minutes.
>
> Choose class, subject, chapters, marks, and difficulty. TeacherEase builds the paper, formats it, and gives you a print-ready PDF.

### Product archetype
TeacherEase should be positioned as:
- AI question paper generator
- handwritten paper conversion workflow
- print-ready paper authoring tool

Not only as a credit-based scan utility.

## Phase roadmap

### Phase 1: Fix conversion and product story (next 2–4 weeks)
#### Workstreams
- Rewrite the homepage hero and CTAs around “generate a paper.”
- Reframe the product into three paths: generate, scan, build manually.
- Make the first-use flow obvious after login.
- Update pricing copy to talk in paper outcomes, not credits.
- Add a demo/sample paper before or during onboarding.

#### Deliverables
- Homepage hero copy, CTA, and product section update.
- Better onboarding copy and a clearer “what do you want to make today?” screen.
- Pricing page language aligned to teacher outcomes.
- Analytics event tracking for signup → first paper → first PDF → repeat paper.

#### Success metrics
- Increase first-paper completion rate.
- Increase percentage of users who reach preview/download.
- Improve time from signup to first meaningful paper creation.

### Phase 2: Unlock the actual paid workflow (next 4–8 weeks)
#### Workstreams
- Add AI paper generation flow for class + subject + chapters + marks + difficulty.
- Support question paper generation that can be edited before export.
- Add answer key and marking scheme generation.
- Add syllabus/PDF upload for chapter-grounded generation.
- Add PDF/DOCX export as the default output.

#### Deliverables
- “Generate Paper” mode in the product.
- Teacher workflow with board/class/subject/chapter/marks selection.
- AI-generated paper + answer key flow.
- Saved generated paper history.

#### Success metrics
- Increase repeat usage after first paper.
- Increase conversion among users who complete at least one paper.
- Increase average value per paid teacher.

### Phase 3: Build retention and reuse (next 2–3 months)
#### Workstreams
- Create a reusable question bank for saved questions.
- Allow teachers to regenerate similar questions by chapter/topic.
- Add paper templates and saved school style presets.
- Support repeat history and “create next paper” flows.

#### Deliverables
- Reusable question library.
- Stored templates and past papers.
- Repeat-paper workflows.

#### Success metrics
- Increase second-paper creation rate.
- Increase monthly active teachers.
- Increase average number of papers per active teacher.

### Phase 4: Expand into schools (later)
#### Workstreams
- Shared school question banks.
- Teacher and admin accounts.
- Approval workflow and shared templates.
- School branding and bulk usage controls.

#### Deliverables
- Multi-teacher org accounts.
- Administrative controls and analytics.

#### Success metrics
- School account activation.
- Paid school adoption.

## Working principles
- Do not change the whole product on day one. First fix the value proposition and workflow.
- Keep manual builder and scan features, but make them support the AI generation story instead of being the main story.
- Sell in “papers,” not “credits,” even if credits remain the technical implementation.
- Prefer direct teacher interviews and behavior data over feature sprawl.

## Immediate execution steps
1. Update the homepage so the hero promises a full paper generation workflow.
2. Add clear “Generate paper / Scan paper / Build manually” CTA structure.
3. Update the pricing section so it speaks in paper outcomes and a free starter offer.
4. Instrument the key funnel: signup → first paper → preview → download → second paper → payment.
5. Interview the strongest existing users to understand what they value and what would make them pay.

## Recommended KPI dashboard
- Signup-to-first-paper conversion
- First-paper-to-download conversion
- Download-to-second-paper conversion
- Free-user-to-paid conversion among teachers who created at least 2 papers
- Average created questions per active user
- Most common usage mode: generate / scan / manual

## Acceptance of this plan
This plan is considered successful when TeacherEase clearly communicates a “question paper generator” story, users can complete a first paper without confusion, and the product is structured around repeat usage rather than per-scan credits alone.
