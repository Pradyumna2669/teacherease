## 1. App Details
- **App name:** PaperGen — Question Paper Maker
- **Short description (80 chars max):**
  Make school exam papers in minutes. Scan a handwritten paper or type any language.

- **Full description:**
Set a question paper in minutes instead of an evening.

Photograph a handwritten paper and PaperGen types it up for you — questions, marks, ruled answer space and all. Or build one from scratch with 23 question types: fill in the blanks, MCQ, match the following, vertical sums, reading comprehension, picture composition and more.

Works in 22 Indian languages. Type in ordinary English letters and the script appears as you go, so you do not need a Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Bengali, or Telugu keyboard.

Every paper exports as a clean A4 PDF that prints and photocopies exactly as you saw it on screen.

Free to start — 3 free papers on signup, no credit card needed.

Key Features:
• AI Handwritten Paper Scanner: Turn rough written questions into print-ready digital exams.
• 23 Exam Question Formats: Fill in blanks, MCQs, match pairs, diagram questions, short/long answers.
• 22 Indian Languages: Seamless phonetic transliteration for Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Bengali, Telugu, Gujarati, Urdu, and more.
• Teacher-Approved Layouts: Automatic headers, roll numbers, time/marks allocation, and ruled answer spacing.
• One-Click A4 PDF Export: Clean formatting engineered to photocopy perfectly for school exams.

## 3. Data Safety Form (Google Play Questionnaire)
- **Data collected:**
  1. Personal info (Email address): Account setup & authentication (Optional/Required, not shared).
  2. Photos & videos (Photos): Used exclusively when the teacher scans a handwritten test paper for AI extraction.
  3. App activity: User-created question papers and drafts stored in their account.
- **Data security practices:**
  - Data is encrypted in transit over HTTPS (TLS).
  - Users can request account and data deletion at any time.
